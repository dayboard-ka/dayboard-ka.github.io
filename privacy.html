Dayboard privacy

Dayboard is the Kappa Alpha chapter calendar. Last updated November 22, 2026.

What Dayboard stores

  • Your sign-in details: your name, email address and profile picture from Google, or the email you use for a sign-in link.
  • The chapter calendar: events, categories and the exec whiteboard that the chapter's exec board adds.
  • Your personal calendar: events, categories, whiteboard tasks and settings you add for yourself.
  • Usage: the days you opened Dayboard and when you were last active.

Who can see what

  • Your personal calendar is private. Only you can see it, including your whiteboard and which chapter events you've checked off. Exec and the admin can't see it.
  • The chapter calendar is visible to every signed-in member. Only exec members can change it.
  • The admin (the chapter member who runs Dayboard) can see the member list (names, emails, roles) and usage: how many people use Dayboard and when each person was last active.

What Dayboard doesn't do

  • No ads, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the chapter.
  • Dayboard only asks Google for your name, email and profile picture. It can't read your Gmail, Drive or Google Calendar.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase, a database service, in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Supabase and, if you choose it, Google.

Leaving

You can download your personal calendar anytime from your account menu. To have your account and personal calendar deleted, contact the chapter's Dayboard admin or email liteonthescript@gmail.com.
