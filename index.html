M month · W week · A agenda · N new event · B whiteboard
Whiteboard: type a task and press Enter. Drag it onto a day to schedule it, or check it off to send it to Completed. Drag it back out of Completed if you misclick. Star up to 3 as today’s focus. Drag any event from the calendar onto the whiteboard to unschedule it.
Month view: click anywhere in a day to open it here. Click an event in this panel to open, edit or check it off.
Double-click a day to add an event. Drag any event (here or on the calendar) onto another day to move it.
Use Manage in the category bar to rename, recolor or delete categories.
Week view: swipe sideways on the trackpad, or pick a week from the strip.
